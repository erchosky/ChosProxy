## Findings fixed during the milestone

| Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Paused messages were bounded in count (500) but not in bytes: up to 4 GiB could be held | Total byte budget of 256 MiB; beyond it messages pass through |
| Low | A request paused while its client disconnected stayed in the queue forever | Client disconnect is watched while paused; the item is removed and the exchange recorded with that cause |

## Known limitations

* "Send to Repeater/Comparer/Organizer" arrive with those tools (M4/M6).
* Intercept rules are host/method/type based; scope rules come with M5.
* Responses are paused only for HTTP/1.1 (HTTP/2 interception is M21).
